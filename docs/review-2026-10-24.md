## Open: needs an owner decision

- **The 15 W corner is not reachable at a 3.0 V cell (Major).** 5.6 A ignores ~72 mOhm typ (~98 max) of series
  resistance (3 x CSD13202Q2, F1, BATFET 19.5 mOhm typ, copper, wires). I x (3.0 - I R) = 15 W / 0.885 gives ~6.8 A and
  VSYS ~2.5 V: above BQ25606 IBATOP 6 A continuous and at IBATFET_OCP 6.0 A min (latches the board dark until USB), and at
  the TPS61088-Q1's 2.5 V max UVLO. In practice 15 W needs a cell above about 3.3-3.4 V. Options: derate the budget to
  about 12 W at low battery (e.g. a higher R18 lowers the 5 V switch limit), or accept a low-battery latch-off and
  document it. Requirements s4 and power-design s8 carry the 5.6 A figure.
- **Off-state drain target < 50 uA is not met (Minor).** BQ25606 battery-only IQ is 58 uA typ / 85 max (SLUSCK6C s8.5),
  plus ~27 uA elsewhere (U5 FB divider ~13 uA, Q1 gate divider 4, VBAT divider 3, HY2113 3). ~85 uA typ is ~0.37 Ah in
  6 months of a 6.5 Ah pack, so "survives months on a shelf" still holds: change the number or accept it.
- **Input limit vs the source's advertised current (Minor).** ILIM is 1.92 A whatever the charger offers; a 9 V / 1.67 A
  PDO or a 5 V Default/1.5 A source is overdrawn (VINDPM does not protect a 9 V source). Accept, or R2 ~330 R (~1.45 A).
- **No ESD on USB-C CC1/CC2 (Minor).** They go straight to the CH224Q, which states no ESD rating. Two low-capacitance
  TVS with >= 20-24 V standoff (CC can meet VBUS in a faulty cable) at J1.

## Open: layout follow-ups (not done here)

- **U6 C_OUT ground (Major, remaining half of #4).** C34's ground still returns to pin 8 through In1; a top-layer path
  would cross the VOUT track, and flipping C34 pushes +3V3 to J8 across the B.Cu EN trunk at x 129. Needs a re-placement
  of C34 / J8 / the EN lane as its own layout stage. Bench-check 3V3 ripple near VSYS = 3.3 V (buck-boost mode).
- Single 0.3 mm vias carry 2.9-3.2 A (BAT into U2, VSYS at U2, L2's feed). ~1.3 mOhm / ~11 mW each with pours at both
  ends: not a thermal limit (checklist s2), but more vias are free where they fit (Minor).
- VBUS reaches U2 pin 24 through a 0.25-0.3 mm track via the VAC pad (16 mV at 2 A; Minor).
- 3V3 output: U6 pin 6 to C34 is a 0.3 mm dog-leg at 1.5 A (Minor).
- Some small bypass caps are 2.5-3.5 mm from their pins (C37 U4 VIN, C12 U4 VCC, C25 U5 VCC, C7 U2 REGN; Minor).

## Open: bench checks added by this review

- Reversed pack **with USB plugged in**: Q1's gate is referenced to BAT, which the charger drives; Q1 may conduct ~90 mA
  in its linear region (~0.5 W) depending on the HY2113's state at VDD-VSS ~1-1.5 V. "Nothing conducts" (s3) is shown
  only without VBUS. Test on a current-limited source.
- VSYS carries ~60-65 uF effective against the BQ25606's "<= 20 uF" compensation (power-design s2 estimates 25-30):
  load step while charging.
- 12 V start-up with the real LCD: COUT1 as built (2 x 4.7 uF 0805, ~2.5-3 uF at 12 V) makes the COUT2 <= 10 x COUT1
  inrush rule marginal.
- 5 V switch-on with the real load (C32 change above).

## Other notes

- No NTC: solder a 10 k resistor across J4-J5 in its place (charging is otherwise suspended: TS sits at 85.5 % of REGN,
  above VT1). Requirements s10 promises a fixed-resistor footprint; the NTC pads serve.
- Requirements s10 says the low-battery cutoff is 2.90 V; HY2113-MB1A's VDL is 2.80 V. Board is 52 x 36 mm vs the
  <= 45 x 30 target (gap 25).
- R4 5.23 k (C25907) has 59 in stock: enough for 5 boards, re-check before ordering. 39 of 58 unique parts are
  Extended (loading fees).
- Checked and fine: every IC pin map and suffix against its datasheet, divider and current-limit values, protector trip
  and fuse coordination, inductor saturation margins, CH224Q VHV/VBUS/CFG1, BQ25606 TS divider (s9.3.5.4), VSET, inner
  layers (no signal tracks on In1/In2), all six TI parts ACTIVE (`part_gate.py`).
